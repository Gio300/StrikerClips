# Store Privacy Declaration Working Set

This is the conservative disclosure set for the current TKO.cam mobile build.

## Collected and linked to the user

- Contact information: email address
- Identifiers: account/user ID and username
- User content: uploaded videos, photos, profile content, posts, comments, messages, and support content
- Purchases: purchase history and entitlement status for activity performed on the web service
- Usage data: product interaction, competition activity, rankings, follows, clans, and predictions
- Diagnostics: crash and performance information when supplied by the platform or hosting provider

## Purposes

- App functionality and account authentication
- User-generated content hosting and social features
- Personalization of profiles, feeds, rankings, and recommendations
- Analytics, security, fraud prevention, moderation, and customer support

## Not used for

- Third-party advertising tracking
- Selling personal data
- Cross-app tracking
- Real-money gambling or cash wagering

## Security and controls

- Data is encrypted in transit
- Users can request or complete account deletion in the app
- Community content supports reporting and blocking
- Child-directed use is not intended; account creation requires age 13 or older
